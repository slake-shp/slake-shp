## Who am I?
My name is Sian and I am a GIS Analyst who loves to work with data and create interesting outputs for clients as well as for fun projects! I have a BSc (Hons) in Geography and a MSc in Geographical Information Science and am also a qualified Lowland Leader who volunteers with the Duke of Edinburgh Award. I think it is fair to say I enjoy maps and learning about the world around me. 🗺️

## Fun Facts
1. I love animals, nature and somewhat controversially in the Geography realm - **history**! 
2. I got way too excited when finding a mushroom whilst on my BSc dissertation fieldwork. :joy:
3. I have met Prince Edward whilst being awarded for contributions to the DofE scheme when I was invited to watch the Birmingham Commonwealth Games in 2022. 
4. I got my drone licence so I could get raw data to use in my BSc dissertation because I was fascinated by not just using but also the collection of data and because I fell in love with remote sensing on top of GIS.
5. I am a *Burgess of Stafford, UK* (the town I was born and raised in) along with every member of my family over the age of 18. It cost a whole 5 pence! I had to swear an Oath to the Mayor of Stafford and the Crown.
